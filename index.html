Handle with Care
Diploma Fall 2025
AHO Oslo

Diploma Project

For decades, Oslo has relied on temporary, modular units to meet kindergarten capacity demands. These modules are now vacant — obsolete structures awaiting demolition.

This diploma argues that these vacant buildings could hold potential of becoming meaningful alternatives: community kindergartens that are rooted in place rather than interchangeable. The project transforms a 1968 wardrobe pavilion at Ekeberg into a kindergarten for Atriet Barnehage.

Along Stone Fences
Hoddevik, Norway
AHO Studio

Project

Located at the end of the road in Hoddevik, Norway, this architectural project is designed as a destination in the landscape. The project incorporates the intimate outside spaces of Madeiran architecture and emphasizes sustainable material use.

At its core, the project fosters a sense of community, offering a place where locals and visitors can converge. In collaboration with Brage Hognestad Hasler.
